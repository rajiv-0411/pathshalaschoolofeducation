Age norms shown here follow common CBSE practice — please confirm exact cut-off dates with the school office at the time of application.

Admission process

How to apply

  1. Enquiry and school visit

    Contact the school office or visit in person to know more about the school and available seats.

  2. Application form

    Collect and fill the admission form from the school office, or submit an enquiry through this website.

  3. Document submission

    Submit the completed form along with the required documents listed below.

  4. Interaction

    An age-appropriate interaction or readiness check may be arranged for the child.

  5. Confirmation

    On confirmation of the seat, admission is completed with payment of applicable fees.

Documents required

What to bring with the application

  • Child's birth certificate
  • Aadhaar card copy (child and parent/guardian)
  • Passport-size photographs of the child
  • Address proof
  • Transfer certificate and latest report card, if applying from another school

Fees

Fee details vary by class and are shared directly by the school office at the time of admission. Please contact us for the current fee structure.
