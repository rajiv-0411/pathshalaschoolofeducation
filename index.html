About us

A trusted name in early and primary education

Founded in 2021, Pathshala School of Education has grown into a dependable school for young learners in Kadamkuan, Patna. Following the CBSE curriculum in an English-medium setting, we focus on building strong foundations — academic, social and personal — for every child who walks through our doors, from Play Group through Class 8.

More about the school

Our vision

To nurture confident, curious and compassionate learners who are well prepared for the world ahead of them.

Our mission

To provide a safe, encouraging classroom where every child is known by name, supported at their own pace, and guided towards good values along with strong academics.

Why families choose us

A school built around the young learner

CBSE-based learning

A structured curriculum aligned with CBSE, building steadily from Play Group through Class 8.

English medium

All teaching and communication is in English, helping children build confidence early.

Small, attentive classrooms

Teachers who know each child individually, with attention given at every stage of learning.

Holistic growth

Co-curricular activities and school events that support learning beyond the textbook.
