Facilities

A campus set up around how young children learn, move and grow through the day.

Bright, spacious classrooms

Well-lit, well-ventilated classrooms sized for close teacher attention at every stage, from Play Group to Class 8.

Library and reading corner

Age-appropriate books and reading time built into the week to build a lasting habit of reading.

Computer basics lab

Introductory computer classes that build comfort with technology alongside the regular curriculum.

Play and activity area

Outdoor and indoor play space for physical activity, sport and structured games through the week.

Health and safety

First-aid support and a monitored campus, with staff attentive to each child's wellbeing through the day.

Transport

School transport arrangements are available on select routes — speak with the office for details.

Art, craft and music

Regular periods for creative expression, alongside the core academic timetable.

Parent-teacher connect

Scheduled parent-teacher meetings to keep families updated on each child's progress.

Want to see the campus in person?

We're happy to have you visit and walk you through our classrooms and facilities.
