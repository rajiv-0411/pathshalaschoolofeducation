How we teach

Learning that stays with children

Lessons at Pathshala combine the structure of the CBSE curriculum with an activity-based approach, so that children understand concepts rather than only memorise them. Classes are kept attentive and manageable, so teachers can respond to how each child is actually learning.

  • Activity and example-based teaching, not just textbook instruction
  • Regular, low-pressure assessment to track each child's progress
  • Dedicated periods for art, physical education and computer basics
  • Parent-teacher meetings to keep families involved in a child's progress
