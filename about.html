About Pathshala School of Education

An English-medium, CBSE-Based Curriculum school in Kadamkuan, Patna, educating children from Play Group to Class 8 since 2021.

Our story

Built for the earliest years of learning

Pathshala School of Education was established in 2021 in Kadamkuan, Patna, with a clear purpose — to give young children a caring, well-structured start to their education. Over the years, the school has grown steadily, staying focused on what matters most in the early years: strong foundations, individual attention and a genuine love for learning.

Today, Pathshala follows the CBSE curriculum and teaches entirely in English, serving students from Play Group through Class 8. The school continues to be guided by the belief that every child learns differently, and deserves a classroom that recognises that.

Our vision

What we work towards

To nurture confident, curious and compassionate learners who are well prepared for the world ahead of them.

Our mission

How we work towards it

  • Build strong foundational skills in literacy, numeracy and language from the earliest years.
  • Create a safe, encouraging classroom where every child is known and supported individually.
  • Follow the CBSE curriculum through an English-medium approach that prepares children for the years ahead.
  • Encourage curiosity, discipline and good values alongside strong academics.

What we stand for

The values behind every classroom

Care

Every child is known by name and supported through their own pace of learning.

Curiosity

Questions are welcomed, and learning is encouraged to go beyond the textbook.

Discipline

A structured, dependable routine helps children feel secure and focused.

Community

Teachers, students and parents work together as one school family.

From the principal's desk

Every child who joins Pathshala brings their own pace, curiosity and promise. Our purpose is simple — to give each one a classroom where they feel safe, seen and encouraged to grow, and to build habits of learning that will stay with them well beyond their years here.
